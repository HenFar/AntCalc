# Research and historical material

This area preserves research history outside the current implementation.

- `session_notes/`: historical working notes.
- `evidence/`: existing release-acceptance and audit evidence, with its original
  dates and provenance.
- [2026-10-07 restructure verification](evidence/restructure-2026-10-07/README.md):
  real runcard comparisons and current Kira reference checks.
- `wolfram/retired-verification/`: retired verification entrypoints.
- `wolfram/src_legacy_flat_2026-06-14/`: the archived flat Wolfram source tree.

Archived source is retained for provenance and is not loaded by the runner.
Paths embedded in historical notes and evidence may describe the former
layout. Maintained Wolfram regressions and runtime basis generators remain
in [legacy/wolfram/dev/](../legacy/wolfram/dev/) beside the package they exercise.
